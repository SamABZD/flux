# Product

Flux is a responsive financial portfolio application built around fictional multi-currency accounts. It demonstrates account history, transfers, currency exchange, cards, payment controls, analytics, budgets, and subscription tracking.

## Positioning

Your money, in focus.

## Experience

The interface should feel calm, direct, and trustworthy. Copy explains financial outcomes in plain language and clearly labels simulated balances, cards, rates, and payments.

Navigation keeps the main actions close: Home, Payments, Analytics, Cards, Accounts, Transactions, Budgets, and Subscriptions. Detail pages preserve their originating filters and provide a clear route back.

## Principles

- Make the next action obvious.
- Keep money and status information precise.
- Treat mobile as a complete layout.
- Preserve context when moving between summaries and details.
- Use color to communicate state, never as the only signal.
- Label every fictional or illustrative boundary.
- Support keyboard navigation, reduced motion, and small-screen reflow.

## Boundaries

Flux uses one shared fictional portfolio. It does not connect to banks, issue cards, authenticate real customers, or move money.
